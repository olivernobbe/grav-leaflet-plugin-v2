Third-party licenses for legacy marker image assets
===================================================

The following image files are retained from the plugin's original Leaflet
marker integrations. Current V2 marker rendering does not reference them, but
they are included in the distribution for compatibility and provenance.

Leaflet.ExtraMarkers
--------------------
Assets: images/markers_default.png, images/markers_default@2x.png,
images/markers_shadow.png, images/markers_shadow@2x.png.
Source: https://github.com/coryasilva/Leaflet.ExtraMarkers
The original plugin pinned Leaflet.ExtraMarkers v1.1.0 at commit
c2ac67f7139b1ae07914f3f671f08a0a7b32adc0.
